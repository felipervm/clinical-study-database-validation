01 — DATABASE DESIGN

Requirements must survive implementation.

A lightweight representation of how an observational study's requirements could become eCRF structures and edit checks. Clinical assumptions here are invented for demonstration, not a real study protocol.

IC / 01

Informed consent

Subject ID and consent date. Used for chronological validation against study visits.

DM / 02

Demographics

Age at baseline and an enrollment descriptor, with an illustrative eligibility constraint.

SV / 03

Study visits

Baseline, Day 14 ±3 and Day 28 ±3, checked against recorded visit dates.

VS / 04

Vital signs

Systolic blood pressure and heart rate, with missing-value and plausibility queries.

A good EDC design doesn't just collect data. It preserves the ability to explain why a value was accepted, flagged or corrected.
